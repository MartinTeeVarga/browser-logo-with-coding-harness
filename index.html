About Logo

      
A browser-based Logo interpreter, with an AI collaborator to help you draw and learn.

      
Logo is a programming language for exploring ideas through code. Give a turtle instructions to move, turn, and draw; use loops, procedures, and lists to build more complex pictures and discover how programs work.

      
Created by Martin, with programming assistance from OpenAI Codex.
